ZORK: THINGS YOU CAN SAY IN PLAIN ENGLISH
(Zork I, II and III. Make sure "Enhanced language" is on in Settings, F2, item 8.
Hints start switched off, so nothing is given away; turn them on in Settings (item 5) if you want help.
Zork always gets the first try; this only helps when Zork wouldn't understand you.
The full list of everything it understands is at the end of each game's manual.)

FINDING YOUR WAY   (these need Map turned on in Settings)
- which way can i go          (shows every exit and where it leads)
- go back                     (back to the room you just came from)
- go to the kitchen           (walks you to any place you've been, one real move at a time)
- go to the lamp              (walks you to where you last saw it)
- how do i get to the kitchen (tells you the way, so you can walk it yourself)
- where have i been
Zork uses compass directions: NORTH, SOUTH, EAST, WEST, NORTHEAST and so on,
plus UP, DOWN, IN and OUT. There is no left or right.
In a maze, drop something in each room so you can tell them apart.

FINDING THINGS   (these need Map turned on in Settings)
- where is the sword          (where you last saw it, and how long ago)
- what did i drop
- where are my treasures      (Zork I only)

ABOUT YOU AND THIS ROOM
- where am i
- is there anything here
- search the room             (looks around and names everything you can see)
- what am i carrying
- am i hurt
- what is my score
- who am i
- is it dark here

DOING THINGS
- grab the lamp, pick it up, put it down, switch it on
- look at the sword, check the box, peek inside the bag
- take everything, drop everything
- take the lamp and go north   (do several things at once with AND or THEN)
- examine the brass thing      (describe something instead of naming it)
- do it again

TALKING TO CHARACTERS
- talk to the troll, say hello to the robot, greet the old man
- ask the robot to go east     (Zork's own way is: ROBOT, GO EAST)

WHEN YOU'RE STUCK   (these need Hints turned on in Settings)
- what can i do here           (some ideas for this room)
- give me a hint               (hints for places you've been, one small step at a time)
Puzzle questions ("how do I get past the troll?") are never answered;
they point you to HINT, so the game isn't spoiled.

GOOD TO KNOW
- how do i play opens the Help screen (or press F1).
- Typos are fixed for you, and it tells you what it changed: "tkae the lmap" works.
- Please, now, for me, can you and question marks are fine: "can you open the door please?"
- UNDO takes back your last move. A whole GO TO walk counts as one move for UNDO.
